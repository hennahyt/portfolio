More Work

I worked as an event technician, handling lighting, sound mixing, and other technical aspects of live events. The events mainly consisted of music gigs and art exhibitions.

In addition to technical production, I worked as a graphic designer, creating social media content and other materials. This included designing posters and other visual assets for Oranssi's events.

I was also responsible for updating and maintaining Oranssi's website using WordPress.

I worked as a production assistant before and during the event, also supporting technical event setup and operations when needed.
I was additionally responsible for supervising a small group of interns, working alongside two other production assistants.

I created printed and physical design materials for the ARKI 135 hub, including laser-cut graphic elements, plywood signage, and presentation cards for the displayed products. I used Adobe Illustrator and InDesign to create the designs and prepare them for production.

I designed the website structure with a focus on usability and user experience, and created wireframes that were also applied in other projects.
I implemented client-provided content, including text and images, and structured it to align with the overall site layout.
The project was built using Figma for wireframing and WordPress for content management and implementation.

I designed logo and colour scheme for AURI2027 project and created a graphic charter to define its visual identity and ensure consistent use across different materials and platforms.

I designed printed materials (flyers, etc.) for Metropolia’s International Services/Relations in accordance with Metropolia’s Visual Guidelines.

I worked as a producer on Loviisa 2018 short film project, focusing mainly on social media and promotion alongside two other team members. We managed social channels, promoted the film, and documented the production during filming. The overall production spanned a few months, including around 1,5 weeks of shooting.

I worked as a production assistant and lighting technician on Matka 2018 short film project. During pre-production, I supported planning and preparation work (location scouting, casting, etc.) ahead of the shooting days. On set, I mainly worked as part of the lighting team, handling lighting setup and adjustments during filming.
