Auntie Solutions

Service Design

2023

This project explored how to ensure a successful implementation of Auntie Solutions from the employee’s perspective.

We conducted benchmarking and an information architecture (IA) audit to identify best practices and potential communication challenges. Based on our findings, we developed a communication plan to support clear and effective implementation.

Our key recommendations included a stronger emphasis on the service’s unique selling point (USP), more strategic timing of communication based on work patterns and activity data, and clearer segmentation of the website supported by IA improvements. We also suggested developing more engaging and accessible additional content, such as audio materials, to improve approachability.
